What is it?

Exchange the company car for a flexible, tax-free budget

The federal mobility budget lets employees swap their company car, or their right to one, for a budget equal to the car's Total Cost of Ownership. Bikes, public transport, shared mobility, housing, even cash: they spend it on the mobility that fits their life. For you, the switch is cost-neutral.

Key fact: spending under pillars 1 and 2 is exempt from income tax and social contributions, and the switch adds no extra cost for you. It replaces the company car budget you already have.

The 3 pillars

One budget, three ways to spend it

Your employees get one budget and total freedom to mix the three pillars however they like.

Pillar 1: A greener car

Keep a company car inside the budget, as long as it meets the legal eco criteria.

  • Fully electric vehicles (mandatory for cars ordered from 2026)
  • Transitional rules for low-emission cars ordered earlier
  • Same comfort, smaller footprint

Pillar 2: Sustainable mobility & housing

The pillar employees love most, tax-free spending on real life.

  • (E-)bikes, e-steps, cargo bikes + equipment & maintenance
  • Public transport, incl. family subscriptions & intl. trains
  • Shared mobility, car pooling, taxis, 30-day car rental
  • Kilometre allowance for cycling or walking
  • Housing: rent or mortgage within 10 km of work, or 50%+ WFH

Pillar 3: Cash out

Whatever isn't spent lands on the January payslip, never lost.

  • Free of income tax
  • Only a 38.07% solidarity contribution deducted
  • Paid automatically, incl. mid-year leavers

Eligibility

Are you and your employees eligible?

For employers

Your company must have made one or more company cars available to at least one employee for an uninterrupted period of at least 36 months. Younger companies qualify as soon as the first car is allocated, "companies in difficulty" are exempt.

For employees

Anyone who has a company car, or is entitled to one under your car policy, can opt in. The old waiting period is abolished, eligible employees switch immediately.

Calculation

How much budget? It starts with the TCO

The budget equals the Total Cost of Ownership of the company car, lease or purchase, fuel, insurance, maintenance, taxes, depreciation, via the actual-costs or flat-rate formula, applied consistently company-wide.

⅕

The 1/5 rule

A mobility budget can never exceed 20% of the employee's gross annual salary. This is the hard cap on every budget.

The legal minimum

The budget can't fall below the indexed statutory minimum, adjusted every year. Skipr's TCO calculator does the math for you, no spreadsheets, no guesswork.
